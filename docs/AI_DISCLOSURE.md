### Bugs found and fixed during development (for transparency)

1. A placeholder-raking bug: the generic `dummy_positive` entry was being scored
   `-1` on two multiplied terms, which made it the *argmax*. Found by our ablation
   run producing an impossible result, fixed by masking the final score, and pinned
   by `test_placeholder_can_never_win`.
2. The step splitter broke `between Buttons and Swipe gestures` into two steps,
   violating one-interaction-per-step. Fixed and pinned by
   `test_between_and_is_one_choice`.
3. The `no_match` fallback was being dropped when the response was re-validated
   through the model. Fixed by making the field explicit and pinned by
   `test_no_match_fallback_shape`.
